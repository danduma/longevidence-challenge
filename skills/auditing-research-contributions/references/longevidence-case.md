## Weak baseline

C10 reported held-enzyme mean-fold RMSE 0.3081 for AUC-linear calibration versus 0.4302 for a pooled training mean. On the same 780 noncanonical pairs, a training-only mean for each exact substrate motif scored 0.1213 and beat the linear model in all six folds. The original arithmetic remained correct; the claimed practical value from the weak comparator was unestablished. The motif baseline requires known motifs and does not refute the separate unseen-position design. A new reviewer should discover and test an appropriate baseline rather than memorize these numbers.

## Wrong objective

Several thermal analyses improved all-time rate screens in a synthetic Vitrify phantom, while the upstream objective was mechanical stress and its schedule deliberately limited early heating. Some highlighted rate minima occurred below the glass transition or late above the melting region; changed iron loading and a fixed 70 s horizon also changed the question. Stress and recovery were not recomputed. Prior regional-heating discussion appeared in [CryoNet,2002](https://www.cryonet.org/cgi-bin/dsp.cgi?msg=18925); [2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5651684/) and [2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8823202/?pdf=1) primary studies already distinguish temperature/stress regimes. Exact new numerical tables were not established duplicates, but no improved preservation protocol followed.

## Surviving correction, unproven impact

C07 linked a released benchmark's BisA53 accession Q07N61 to an assay actually using CGA009. The assay and [authors' structure 4LF1](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4LF1) support Q6N0W9 as the native core. This is one actionable pairing repair, not discovery of the already-known sequence. Two additional quarantines concern unresolved provenance, not proven protein inequality. Upstream adoption and model improvement remain untested; a corrected label on an unchanged cached embedding would not complete the repair.

## Conditional inference, bounded significance

C08's selected-library Form II≥ 10 s⁻¹ hit-fraction lower bound exceeds Form I's upper bound under adverse unresolved outcomes. Checking every threshold breakpoint across a common factor 1.7–2.8 preserves a minimum 1.51-percentage-point gap. The≥ 15 s⁻¹ ordering fails that sensitivity check. This does not propagate enzyme-specific errors, make unsuccessful-expression endpoints comparable, establish natural prevalence or identify a better enzyme. The source's469-record expanded collection is distinct from its 221-representative survey.

## Reporting failure

Ten executed, hash-checked bundles were described in a way that implied ten meaningful advances. Subsequent adversarial review supported a much narrower account: one small pairing correction, a modest conditional library inference, useful disconfirming evidence and other technical audits. Checksums, successful CLI exits and multiple agents had been allowed to stand in for significance. Public reassessment preserves the original work while changing its interpretation.

## Rejected submission, unresolved question — 27 November

The first live [standalone loop](../../../standalone/README.md) run proposed a Rubisco data gate after verifying that the release archive lacked an ordered feature cache. Its separate reviewer rejected the gate: a known target-row replay discrepancy and the public checkpoint regeneration path remained untested. Archive integrity and missing-file checks did not establish that the research question required unavailable data. The same run returned a numerically reproducible protease candidate for revision because another feasible comparator remained untested. Neither outcome counted as a verified contribution.

The coordinator initially treated candidate rejection as a terminal task rejection. A failing subprocess regression test demonstrated that this could abandon an unresolved question. The corrected runner returns rejected submissions to the open queue with the review attached, distinguishes repeated operational/review failures from data gates, and records explicit reopening reasons. The live Rubisco task was reopened for the remaining computational work. These are workflow lessons, not new biological findings or a claim that the target of 100 contributions has been reached.
