# Lessons from this effort

These are changes to our working method, grounded in failures of this research batch. The reusable instructions are in [auditing-research-contributions](../../skills/auditing-research-contributions/SKILL.md).

1. **Separate execution, correctness, novelty, usefulness and validation.** Hashes and successful reruns address execution. They do not prove the question mattered. Ten bundles were delivered; ten meaningful advances were not demonstrated.
2. **Search outside the selected source and our own agents' reports.** Read original papers, supplements, data, code, issues, later studies and relevant field discussions. CryoNet's2002 regional-heating discussion and2017 mechanical studies materially changed our novelty assessment. Longevity forum searches include LongeCity, Rapamycin.news, Fight Aging!, CryoNet and Biostasis when relevant; distinguish discussions, newsletter mirrors, opinion and experiments. Failed access and missing search hits are not positive evidence.
3. **Challenge the comparator before a large computation.** C10 beat a pooled mean but lost to training-only substrate averages on every held-enzyme panel. Check a domain-informed comparator available under the same information and split constraints. State restrictions explicitly; do not transfer that criticism to genuinely unseen motifs.
4. **Use the source's actual objective and stopping condition.** Raising a temperature-rate floor did not show improvement to Vitrify's stress objective. Increased loading changes its design space. A fixed horizon can continue heating beyond the source's temperature-based endpoint. Trace a claimed pass/failure back to its location, time, temperature and physical mechanism.
5. **Do the analytic sanity check early.** C04's tiny margin is mostly explained by initial power divided by capacity. A sweep may quantify behavior but must add information beyond an obvious identity to justify its cost and significance claim.
6. **Keep the population and endpoint fixed.** A deliberately selected 469-record library is not a representative221-variant survey or a random sample of nature. Undefined or unsuccessful-expression outcomes cannot silently become measured slow enzymes. Test shared calibration uncertainty and explain the individual uncertainty that remains.
7. **A null needs resolution and relevance.** C03's overlapping patches and large spatial discretization changes weaken its general usefulness even though its narrow null is correctly reported. Numerical resolution must support the decision being promoted.
8. **A correction needs a complete evidence chain and a downstream test.** C07 supports one sequence–measurement repair. Existing correct accession knowledge is not our discovery. Embeddings have to be regenerated from the replacement sequence; relabeling a cached vector is invalid. Adoption and improved model performance remain unproven.
9. **Use agents to challenge one another's assumptions.** Other agents rerunning the same code are computational checks, not human review or independent experimental replication. Commission falsification and alternative explanations before promoting findings. Preserve disagreement and unsuccessful checks.
10. **Optimize for a useful result, not a contribution quota.** Stop counting units as achievements when scope or significance changes. Preserve artifacts and credit, publish the downgrade prominently, and choose the next test by the decision it could resolve. Never require a positive result to call a careful investigation complete.

The next effort should start with one credible contribution and a hard test of its value. It should not begin with another request to manufacture ten discoveries.
