# Skill verification record

The new [skill](../../skills/auditing-research-contributions/SKILL.md) was checked against the actual audit cases rather than a claim that its presence guarantees good research.

| Request/example | Expected routing and behavior | Evidence in this effort |
|---|---|---|
|Assess a claimed meaningful gain in a held-enzyme AUC predictor|Load the skill; test feasible domain-informed training-only comparators and state the held-out information boundary|`verify.py` independently recalculates motif means/medians, checks every test motif is available in training, retains signed estimates and reproduces the omitted-baseline counterexample.|
|Assess Form II versus FormI hit fractions with missing outcomes|Load the skill; preserve population/endpoint, test shared calibration separately from individual errors|Exact rational breakpoint/interior enumeration preserves the10 s⁻¹ ordering and defeats the15 s⁻¹ ordering. The report retains selected-library and latent-endpoint limits.|
|Describe a repaired sequence pairing as improved enzyme prediction|Load the skill; separate source identity, native-core consistency, adoption and downstream value|The public record supports one native-core pairing repair and explicitly leaves embedding regeneration, model impact and upstream acceptance unproven.|
|Use a higher all-time rate to claim lower thermomechanical stress|Load the skill; check the source objective and physical support|Primary2017/2022 evidence and Vitrify's stated objective narrow the claim to a benchmark/objective audit. No stress improvement is asserted.|
|Fix a typo or run ordinary software tests|Do not turn the task into a literature/forum campaign|The skill's scope explicitly excludes ordinary software verification and direct prose corrections.|

This is a retrospective worked-case and trigger review, not a blind prospective efficacy experiment or external expert review. The skill validator additionally checks frontmatter/scaffold validity; it cannot prove scientific judgment. Public and installed copies are compared byte for byte at publication. The source-linked cases make future regressions in reasoning concrete.
