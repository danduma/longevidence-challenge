## Public reproduction and redistribution

`./verify.sh` uses the shared scientific Python, pinned local snapshots and no network or LLM calls. Public systems may set `C07_PYTHON=/path/to/python` (Python3 with numpy2.5.5, pandas3.1.1 and scikit-learn1.9.1 recorded in results/environment.json). `audit.py` requires only local files. `fetch.py` is the optional workspace snapshot/network acquisition script; `fetch_history.py` refreshes deleted accession histories. Refreshes are new observations and will generally differ from frozen hashes.

To acquire the release independently, download each `release_relative_path` in inputs/manifest.json from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jamesyoung93/Rubisco_Protein_Language_Model_Active_Learning/ade87f378aa25c24a24c19915722aa04be92936e/{release_relative_path}`, save under its `file` name and check its recorded SHA256. All evidence URLs and saved paths are recorded in retrieval logs; download those bytes, checking the hashes, or obtain the dated version from UniSave. Mutable live endpoints cannot guarantee retrieval of an identical later snapshot. The verifier checks optional shared paths only if they exist; public reruns do not require that workspace.

Rights: UniProt-derived accession records, released FASTA/metadata and replacement sequence may be redistributed with attribution to the UniProt Consortium under [CC BY4.0, primary UniProt documentation](https://www.uniprot.org/api-documentation/support-data). RCSB/wwPDB deposition data are openly available; original study/release citations should be preserved. The supplied release's LICENSE_DATA grants CC BY4.0 to original generated outputs but expressly excludes third-party datasets. Flamholz's GitHub API reports no repository license; therefore **inputs/assays.csv, paper HTML/PDF/text snapshots and third-party source material are local verification copies, not licensed redistribution artifacts**. Do not redistribute those copies in a public package; provide download URLs and hashes instead. The downloaded crop PDF is unused. Do not infer that an open-access landing page grants redistribution of every file. C07 authored code, patch decisions, result tables and report may be shared with this attribution and source links; source-record eligibility tables derived from the compendium should be treated as local verification artifacts until source reuse terms are established. No external redistribution was performed here.
