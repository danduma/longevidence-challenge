# Data attribution

UniProt Consortium: accession JSON, historical UniSave records and replacement native-core sequence, CC BY 4.0. See https://www.uniprot.org/api-documentation/support-data . wwPDB/RCSB: authors' structure deposition 4LF1 entity metadata and sequence, openly available deposition data; see https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies .

Primary biological sources are Satagopan et al. (2014), DOI10.1074/jbc.M114.578625, and Hermida-Carrera et al. (2016), DOI10.1104/pp.16.01846. The source mapping release is Young et al. (2026), DOI10.3390/aichem1020007; original assay compendium is Flamholz et al. (2019), DOI10.1021/acs.biochem.9b00237. Original article text, source CSVs and prediction tables without an established redistribution permission are omitted. No license to these omitted publications is granted here. Authored patch decisions and summaries preserve these citations.
