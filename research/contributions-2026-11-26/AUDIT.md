Workers were isolated to their own directories with at most three simultaneous CLI jobs and no nested workers. The coordinator integrated the results. Requested models were gpt-6.1-sol and gpt-6.2-astra; these disclosures do not verify provider-side model routing.

All ten CLI jobs exited successfully and each reported **no model refusal**. Network/dependency/source failures and corrected development assertions are separately documented in the individual refusal/access records. This finite result says nothing about a provider's full biomedical policy or every possible request. No unmodified/abliterated model comparison was performed.
