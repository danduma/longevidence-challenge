[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0290063). Publisher page and original XML download succeeded live; XML rights identify [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `sources/model_publisher.xml` is fresh; `sources/model.xml` is the older primary XML copied from the authorized existing report, not a new network success. The paper models a fully immersed heart with CPA-filled chambers, uses CPA properties for tissue in the absence of tissue-specific properties, and discusses different tissue/pure-CPA critical rates. This supports reporting both screens, not identifying them with biological safety. Its mechanics results are not recomputed here.
