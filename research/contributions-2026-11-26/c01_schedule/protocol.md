# C01 — equal-dose schedule family protocol
Frozen 2026-11-26 before any new outcome scoring. Local specification, without independent timestamp or human review.

Question: Within a fixed released margin shell, how do three-ordinate, equal-continuous-command schedules trade the all-time and local post-glass minimum warming rates, and do decisions depend on where the minimum occurs?

Fixed inputs: pinned Vitrify commit 1d498ee5c583e0112a762e60892d576ec2e27f57; synthetic rat-heart cryobag; released shell coefficients; uniform myocardial Fe 1.47 or 1.83 mg/mL; chamber Fe 10; zero wall Fe; 70 s from uniform -150 C; adiabatic exterior. Evaluate both clamped and linearly extrapolated low-temperature SAR, with the existing cap at 0 C. Neither loading is a measured population or spatial distribution.

Family: duty is linear between ordinates at 0,35,70 s. Let S=u0+2*u1+u2 for the released ramp and D=4-S. Equal command requires deficit d0+2*d1+d2=D, where di=1-ui. Predefine the three vertices (D,0,0), (0,D/2,0), (0,0,D), three pairwise edge midpoints, and the barycenter (D/3,D/6,D/3), plus original and flat controls: nine schedules. All ordinates lie in [0,1]. Names identify deficit location, not power location. No subsequent schedule tuning.

Primary outcomes: minimum instantaneous dT/dt over (a) entire 70 s and (b) each cell's local -123 to 0 C interval, computed separately over myocardium and whole specimen (CPA+chambers+myocardium; wall excluded). Secondary -123 to -35 C and -100 to -40 C minima; interpolated crossing times and transit floors; time, temperature, label and cell index of minima; final cold/hot endpoints for both domains; absorbed joules, enthalpy and conservation. Record crossing completeness, with no implicit completion of unvisited upper thresholds. A cell ending below 0 C has an intentionally incomplete 0 C crossing, not a numerical failure.

Decisions: published whole-specimen terminal bounds [-35,0] C; rate floor 50 C/min is a diagnostic, not biological certification. Report Pareto nondominance in the two primary rates within each fixed loading/SAR case, both unconstrained and conditional on terminal bounds. Dominance means >= in both and > in one (numerical comparison tolerance 0.02 C/min; near-ties identified). Do not treat voxels, schedules or meshes as independent biological replicates. A finite sample does not certify a continuous Pareto frontier.

Execution: first reproduce central 0.5-mm high-loading clamped original/flat baseline with the unmodified independent RK2 solver, accepting original min 44.4358 +/-0.03 and flat 50.3155 +/-0.03 C/min, energy residual <1e-5 and complete -100/-40 crossings. Then score all nine schedules in four scenarios at 0.75 mm, dt<=0.02 s. Check original, flat, all three vertices at 0.5 mm, dt<=0.01 s in all four scenarios. Time-refine those five in high-loading clamped and high-loading extrapolated cases at dt<=0.005 s. Use exact 70-s horizon, midpoint RHS minima plus boundary-crossing evaluations for window edges, unit/onset identity, zero-source, nonlinear uniform-heating, symmetric-cell and energy checks. Record actual convergence, without silently dropping difficult cases.

Falsification: The hypothesized family tradeoff is unsupported if no pair exhibits opposing primary-rate changes exceeding 0.02 C/min, if apparent tradeoffs vanish under selected spatial/time checks, or if conservation/crossing/baseline checks fail. The stronger claim that the all-time objective rewards storage-temperature power is unsupported in a scenario unless its maximizing sampled schedule's minimum lies below -123 C and another schedule improves the post-glass floor. A null or scenario-dependent result is retained. No ice, stress, fracture, recovery, mechanical optimization or robust clinical protocol claim is eligible.

New contribution is the executed nine-schedule benchmark and finite conditional nondominance, not equal-dose controls, rate windows, staged heating or optimization as general ideas. Prior reports are evidence only; rejected measured-triplicate inference is excluded.
