# C08 protocol | 26 November 2026

Before scoring new outcomes. Read the accepted synthesis, original report, input headers and paper's study design. The published screen totals and calibration are prior results.

**Question.** Among the 469 Supplementary Data 3 records, how tightly does the deposited 30 °C lab endpoint identify the fraction at or above 10 s⁻¹ (the paper defines slow as below 10), and can form-level frequency orderings survive unrestricted missingness? Secondary endpoint ≥15 s⁻¹ uses the paper's stated fast range. Include every record once, including controls, prior work and follow-up entries. No literature rates, predictions, weighting by assay N, population extrapolation or sampling confidence intervals.

**Comparisons.** Reconcile source row identity, flags and numeric values against the prior ledger and lab subset of the master table. Use deposited form, prok/euk and new-to-study categories. Report numeric coverage, endpoint ascertainment, numeric-only frequency H/M, ascertained frequency H/(M+C), confirmed yield H/N and sharp finite-library bounds [H/N,(H+U)/N], where C is explicitly active below 0.5 and U has no lab rate or threshold flag. Verify flag conventions and condition on the corrected reported means. Report if eligibility, units, flag scale or row identity are ambiguous. Do not infer individual expression failure from a blank. If a latent comparable rate exists for each blank, bound the full median by taking the median of the componentwise lower and upper endpoints of its possible rates (nonnegative, no finite maximum for blanks). Treat a censoring ceiling as a supremum. Include numeric-only and ascertained medians. Show all pairs of forms for both thresholds; strict separation of bounds identifies an ordering, otherwise report possible ties or reversals. Use no outcome-based choice of forms.

**Falsification.** An independent reconstruction with these keys and definitions must return the same counts and bounds. An ordering deemed forced is falsified by an admissible assignment eliminating its strict advantage. A claim of ambiguity fails if the sign is invariant across all completions. Unreconciled keys or source evidence that blanks encode a kinetic inequality invalidate the affected calculation. No inference of intrinsic rates for enzymes lacking a well-defined comparable assay endpoint.
