# C03 protocol — frozen before new scoring, 2026-11-26

Question: in the pinned synthetic Vitrify phantom, do compact myocardial low-iron patches with equal deficit mass produce materially different post-glass transit or terminal cold spots solely through placement?

Fixed model: released margin shell and ramp, nominal muscle mean 1.47 mg Fe/mL, chamber 10, clamped SAR, adiabatic exterior, −150 °C initial state, 70 s exact horizon, independent finite-volume midpoint RK2. Discovery spacing 0.75 mm; checks 0.50 mm. Baseline first: reproduce archived 0.50-mm uniform 1.83 clamped ramp (rate, window rate, terminal extrema within 1e-8).

Patch volumes targeted 0.010 and 0.040 mL, rounded to nearest voxel count; all three placements at a given spacing/size use exactly that count. Local deficit amplitude 0.36 mg Fe/mL; remaining muscle receives +0.36*N/(M−N), preserving mean 1.47 and total iron exactly to numerical precision. Patch is the N myocardial voxel centers nearest a fixed geometric seed (ties by voxel index). Seeds: chamber-adjacent, deep, exterior-adjacent. To avoid outcome selection, seed selection uses geometry only: among muscle voxels with y≥center_y and |x−center_x|≤1 mm and |z−center_z|≤1 mm, chamber seed minimizes distance to chamber, exterior seed minimizes distance to surrounding CPA, deep seed maximizes minimum of both distances; ties favor nearest (center_x, center_y+3.5, center_z), then index. Distances are Euclidean grid distances. Report actual coordinates, connectivity, distances and overlap; localized does not imply disjoint. Same selection rule at both spacings; grid-induced seed changes are a limitation.

Comparators: uniform nominal; uniform 1.47−0.36*N/M (equal mean to uncompensated patch, a lower-total-iron control); all three compensated placements. Two sizes separate placement from size effects, but deficit mass increases with size; no claim of pure size effect at fixed mass. Primary placement contrasts: deep minus chamber and deep minus exterior at 0.040 mL. Refine all three large patches and nominal at .50 mm; halve dt .01→.005 for the primary large-patch trio at .50 mm. No optimization or amplitude tuning.

Primary endpoints, separately for myocardium and whole specimen (CPA+muscle+chamber, wall excluded): terminal minimum °C; longest voxel-local transit −123→−35 °C, with incomplete crossings reported rather than imputed. Secondary: −100→−40 transit and minimum instantaneous rate, −123→−35 minimum instantaneous rate, terminal maxima, patch terminal minimum. Transit uses linear crossing interpolation; rates sampled at RK2 stages. All-time minima are diagnostic only, not a discovery endpoint. A whole-specimen cold spot can mask a local effect; report both.

Materiality rule: placement range ≥0.10 °C in terminal myocardial minimum OR ≥0.10 s in longest complete myocardial post-glass transit, with same paired sign at .75 and .50 mm and temporal error less than 10% of paired contrast (or <.01 in endpoint units). Otherwise an informative null or mesh-dependent result. Threshold is a model-resolution convention, not a biological threshold. Falsification: no contrast meeting these rules; sign reversal under refinement; lack of matching iron/count; invalid crossings or unacceptable energy residual (>1e-5). Include source unit check, zero-source preservation and crossing check. No statistical replicates: deterministic cases and voxels are not independent biological samples.

Scope: synthetic delivery maps, not coronary measurements. Prior half-muscle/equal-iron tests and rate-window concepts are prior art. No ice, stress, fracture, recovery or human-expert validation inferred. Protocol is locally recorded, without external preregistration timestamp.
