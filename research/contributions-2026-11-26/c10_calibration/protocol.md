# C10 | Frozen reporter-to-rate calibration protocol

Frozen 2026-11-26 after reading prior reports, the input schema and canonical records, before fitting or scoring. Do published corrected recorder AUCs carry quantitative information about signed relative biochemical initial rates across the six available TEVp panels and across held-out substrate motifs?

Use the assigned common-key CSV unchanged, depth at least 100 in both reporter versions, unique enzyme × motif keys and finite corrected AUC and vinirel. Require the canonical sequence or a single substitution from it. Background columns may be missing. Exclude ENLYFQS from both fitting and scoring in the primary analysis because its response is fixed by normalization. No trimming, averaging over missing labels, log transform, boundary at zero, weights, or target-dependent cutoffs. One observed pair has one vote in fitting.

Fit pooled intercept-plus-AUC ordinary least squares (primary), increasing least-squares isotonic regression with tie pooling, linear interpolation between training AUCs and constant endpoint extrapolation (fixed secondary), and the pooled training-response mean. Use corrected AUC directly without biochemical or reporter anchoring. Never use test outcomes in predicting. Leave each complete enzyme panel out once. Separately leave out all substitutions at one motif position across all enzymes, rotating over seven positions. This blocks identical motifs and their same-position neighbors. For the latter split also score a within-enzyme training mean, since other labels for that enzyme are available.

Primary metric in each design is the arithmetic mean of the held-group RMSEs in canonical-relative rate units. The claim of average improvement in both designs fails if linear fails to beat the pooled training mean in either. Report all fold errors, pooled RMSE, MAE, prediction minus observation bias and descriptive pooled calibration intercept/slope from observed on out-of-fold predicted (ideal 0/1). No winner selection or refit using those calibration slopes. Report group wins and individual exceptions. No row-based confidence intervals or replicate claims.

Normalization audit: repeat enzyme holdout including canonical in training and test and report change. For the position design retain canonical only in training as a sensitivity on identical noncanonical tests. Inspect deposited rate representations of canonical, distinguish repeated unit ratios from replicate measurements, explain denominator dependence and whether absolute or fully control-independent validation is possible. No target-derived test scaling. These are finite selected panels, with shared assay and substrate context.

Before scoring, the pinned release tree also identified data_viniabs_Luzi.txt. Use it only to audit the denominator by comparing each deposited relative estimate with the corresponding initial estimate divided by the canonical entry for that enzyme and position. Check the number of distinct canonical values per panel. Do not use these source slopes as predictors, rescale held-out predictions, or fit an absolute-kinetics calibration.
