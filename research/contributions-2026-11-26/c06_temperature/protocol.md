# C06 — Rubisco temperature-calibration evidence audit

Frozen 2026-11-26 before calculating new temperature-pair outcomes. Existing accepted rank-1 result and prior reports have been read as evidence, not instructions.

Question: do the finite supplied unfiltered historical workbook and its underlying public source tables contain independently measured, same-enzyme temperature series that constrain the Q10 assumed by the 2025 coupled-assay calibration, and does an admissible bridge increase its design rank?

Primary comparisons:
1. Enumerate every supplied workbook row, including excluded, mutant, secondary and missing-temperature rows. Enumerate all within-origin candidate pairs with identical literal species, isoform/variant, expression background, source and recorded non-temperature conditions, differing measured temperatures and positive turnover. Preserve exclusions and uncertain identity rather than merging guessed aliases.
2. Reconcile promising series with original source tables/code. Eligible ratios require documented same enzyme/construct within the source, measured temperatures and turnover in compatible per-active-site units, matched assay origin and known non-temperature conditions. A compilation-only candidate remains unsupported until checked. Specificity pairs do not constrain turnover Q10. Predicted or temperature-normalized values do not count as measurements.
3. Compute R = k(T_high)/k(T_low) and interval-effective Q10 = R**(10/(T_high-T_low)) for eligible endpoints. No temperature imputation, no extrapolation, no assumption of a globally constant Q10, no significance claims from correlated pair counts. Distinguish exact 25→30°C support from wider-temperature intervals and rates from fitted curves.
4. Match each series to all eleven author-defined calibration overlaps with exact accession/construct identity where available. Literal species similarity is a coverage flag only. Tabulate differences in enzyme identity, assay origin, pH, gases, normalization and temperature support; unknown is not equal.
5. Evaluate the model log(L/A)=alpha−(30−T_lit)/10*beta with beta=log(Q10). An independent admissible within-enzyme same-assay temperature contrast adds row [0,delta_T/10]. Report algebraic rank with such rows under a shared-Q10 assumption separately from rank supported for exact calibration enzymes/conditions. A species-only or endpoint-incompatible link is not a validated bridge.

Falsification: a verified matched measured turnover series for an exact calibration enzyme with suitable temperature support would refute a zero-direct-coverage finding; an independent compatible temperature row would raise two-parameter rank to two. Conversely, no verified pairs or only condition/identity-mismatched series refutes a claim that the supplied data independently identify a transferable calibration Q10. Q10=2.2 is not declared wrong solely because it is assumed or because a wider-temperature pair differs.

Scope: exhaustive relative to all rows/sheets of the pinned supplied historical workbook and finite source-table candidates found there, plus explicitly logged targeted public retrievals. No claim of an exhaustive worldwide literature search. No new experiments, contacts, other model calls, subagents or fitting of sequence models. Rows/pairs are not independent biological replicates. Record retrieval failures separately from model refusals. Redistribution requires identified permission; otherwise retain locally for verification and provide public acquisition instructions.

Outputs: executable extraction/verifier, local snapshots with SHA-256 provenance and rights notes, complete row/pair/coverage ledgers, report.md, refusals.md, contribution.json. Offline verification uses the shared scientific Python and pinned local snapshots only.
