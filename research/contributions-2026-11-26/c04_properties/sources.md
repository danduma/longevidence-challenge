# C04 · Sources and reproducibility

Inspected 26 November 2026. The independently implemented thermal RK2 solver and geometry driver come from Longevidence contributors, commit 84eb8fbd51deb9eee7914df1b46161a589c6a8e9. C04 adapts the former to scale source and capacity, changes the transit diagnostic and adds post-glass and myocardial summaries. It specializes the latter to one fixed loading. Original MIT license and third-party notices are included. The geometry and design JSON originate from Marc Davici's Apache-2.0 release at 1d498ee5c583e0112a762e60892d576ec2e27f57. The vendor license and geometry header are retained. [Portable solver and original files](https://github.com/danduma/longevidence-challenge/tree/84eb8fbd51deb9eee7914df1b46161a589c6a8e9/research/cryopreservation) · [Vitrify](https://github.com/Marc-Dvci/Vitrify/tree/1d498ee5c583e0112a762e60892d576ec2e27f57)

To independently acquire the starting materials, download the portable solver directory at that commit. Its vendor/ directory contains geometry, and its inputs/ directory contains grid_convergence_margin.json. The two 0.5_flat/ramp_1.83_0_1_clamped_0.01_0_-150_2.json files in its results/ directory provide the numerical facts extracted to inputs/baselines.csv. The original output hashes in flat/ramp order are 0b60d5b9c1bb8d8f81b183b9b1ef3358ef2d34f0698acef4f0a1ffbe5d1e464d and 58138dd88e343d165e55839e31c1b6772c7ba925bcd8ff80689acbb5bc25007b. The executable uses local snapshots only. inputs.sha256 pins those snapshots and the pre-score protocol. consulted.sha256 identifies the original code, accepted synthesis, historical reports and original XML consulted in the supplied workspace. Downloadable paper XML is available from [Europe PMC, rat heart](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9164386/fullTextXML) and [Europe PMC, constitutive equations](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10431620/fullTextXML).

The SAR curve shape, piecewise thermal properties, and post-glass support follow published heart thermal models. Gao et al. report 690.82 and 415 W/g Fe at two temperatures, under a stated excitation condition. Those are temperatures along a curve. A pointwise replicate uncertainty envelope for this phantom from −150 °C was unavailable in the primary material inspected. Phatak et al. measured specific heat in small DSC pans over temperature, including differing crystallization conditions. That study does not establish a coherent multiplicative uncertainty band in volumetric capacity for chamber, bath and loaded myocardium together. Both C04 multipliers are sensitivity coordinates only. The clamped curve and its published 691 W/g Fe nominal cold value are retained. [Gao et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100873) · [Joshi and Rabin, Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0290063) · [Earlier heart thermal model](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4053105) · [Phatak et al.](https://doi.org/10.1089/bio.2018.0006) · [Phatak thesis, DSC methods and curves](https://conservancy.umn.edu/bitstream/handle/11299/191240/Phatak_umn_0130M_17932.pdf) · [Sharma et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202101691)

Local execution used macOS 26.6.2 arm64, Apple clang 21.0.0 (clang-2100.4.1.1), C++17 -O3, Python 3.14.10, NumPy 2.5.5 and SciPy 1.18.1. Public reproduction requires a C++17 compiler on PATH as c++ and Python with NumPy/SciPy. From this contribution directory run python -B verify.py. It compiles locally, uses a temporary subdirectory here, regenerates results, and checks numerical conclusions with tolerances. No installation or network is required in the specified environment.
