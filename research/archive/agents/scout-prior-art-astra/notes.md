## Decisions and limitations

- The trade-off audit uses the lower of the two historical primary maize rates, hence the more permissive screen. It retains cultivar names and uses equal-weight medians of numeric measurements within publication and label. Missing hard-to-measure kinetic parameters are plausibly nonrandom. The same paper can include multiple pH values or cite older assays, so this join is an upper bound on provenance resolution available from those columns. The site does not require both control species to occur in a single paper. It requires the candidate to be measured against each appropriate control.
- The 2025 cryobag study has three independent samples per condition. Reported precision is across bags. Different volumes, geometry and iron loading preclude an independent estimate of concentration response. Mean warming is insufficient for the lower tail over space. The published cooling model comparisons use the same study as the measurements.
- The random-table null has 20 amino-acid labels, constant degeneracy counts, uniform codons and uniform single-base substitutions. For a random assignment the chance of equal labels at two distinct words is the sum of n×(n−1) over classes, divided by 256×255. This is an expectation over tables. No organism-level conclusion follows from symmetry. In the 2021 ratio check, restriction to UAGA vs UAGG defines a reproducible single contrast; other fourth-base, third-base and triplet controls are present in the source workbook.
- Several publisher pages returned transient redirect/cookie errors, the eLife frontend returned a browser challenge, and Europe PMC and NCBI metadata requests intermittently timed out or returned 503/429. Public archive copies, DOI records and the primary supplements supplied the evidence used. The ACS meeting landing page exposed limited content; the indexed author abstract supports only the stated disclosure. None of these was a model safety refusal.
