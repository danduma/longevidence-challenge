Motivated by the 2026 Table 1 italics and 2023 Supplementary Table 1 (both inspected), check ALL six common kinetic columns in BOTH reused strains, rather than selecting only discrepant endpoints. Preserve each reported number/error string, original error kind and replicate-count range. Compare rounded mean intervals, and ask whether a reported 2023 SD and 2026 SE can obey SD/SE=sqrt(n) for any integer n=3..6 under the full printed rounding intervals. The 2026 Table 1 caption states 3..5 replicates; use the wider 3..6 range conservatively, reporting both. This is a deterministic audit of whether uncertainty can safely be transported as an unchanged source statistic, not a hypothesis test or allegation of incorrect experiments. For typography, the printed decimal places define half-unit rounding intervals. An integer error printed as '1' has half-width 0.5, making this check conservative.
