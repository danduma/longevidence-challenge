# Priorities for computational progress on the Millennium biology problems

Research date: 26 November 2026. **Selected: Improve Rubisco, Cryopreservation, Programmable proteases.** The goal is a useful, falsifiable computational contribution; completing one of the organizers' experimental demonstrations is a much larger task. [Official criteria](https://millenniumproblems.bio/).

Three actual Codex CLI scouts independently examined all twelve from data-first, mechanism-first and skeptical prior-art starting points. Their full reports, source inventories and calculations are retained in `agents/`. They disagree substantially about third place and about whether mechanistic bounds or data-driven discovery are the better use of effort. This decision preserves that disagreement rather than describing the ranking as objective.

## Decision rule and ranking

D = accessible, relevant public data; T = tractability of meaningful additional computation; I = prospective scientific/social impact; V = path to validation of the computational contribution. Scores run 1–5. The coordinator took the **median of the three scouts' judgments for each dimension**, then applied **35% D, 30% T, 20% I, 15% V**. These are ordinal decision judgments, not probabilities or independent experimental estimates. The scouts' validation interpretations differ, and their literature overlaps. ## Primary evidence and practical attacks

**Rubisco.** The [2025 tree-of-life survey](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2501433122), its [public data/code](https://gitlab.com/milo-lab-public/rubisco_is_slow), and [2026 cyanobacterial paired kinetics](https://doi.org/10.1093/plphys/kiag259) provide current evidence. [Phylogenetic constraints](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msab079) and [assay variability](https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erab356) are already known; rediscovering them is not novelty. Attack the problem through a source/condition-aware two-trait frontier, audited sequence linkage, independent sequence/phylogenetic splits, and informative missing-specificity measurements. Existing fast-rate screens without specificity cannot certify a joint candidate. Executed artifacts: `agents/scout-data-sol/results/rubisco_*`, `rubisco/`, and both independent release reviewers. Required later validation is the specified paired enzyme assays.

**Cryopreservation.** [Rat-kidney recovery](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38824-8), [2025 liter-scale physical data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-63483-2), and the [October 2026 human-scale pig-kidney preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.07.756990) are relevant at different validation levels. The latter is not peer reviewed and acute graft function does not establish long-term normal whole-animal recovery. Attack local heating/CPA-delivery uncertainty and validate against withheld thermometry and regional failure observations. Nominal inverse nanowarming design is [already public prior art](https://github.com/Marc-Dvci/Vitrify). Executed source-data audits are in both `scout-mechanisms-sol` and `scout-prior-art-astra`; their numerical results agree and the mechanism scripts were freshly rerun by the coordinator. The [new thermal sensitivity study](archive/agents/cryo-robust-astra/report.md) actually evaluates delivery perturbations and frozen schedules under both SAR assumptions; the coordinator tests broader rate windows as well. The independent Sol review supports the precise new numerical contribution after narrowing its empirical/priority claims. Full coordinator reproduction and four separate-solver cases pass. These are computational counterexamples, not a robust uncertainty model validated against tissue outcomes.

**Proteases.** [ProtRec/MLDEEP](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-60622-7) supplies a large screen, controls and selected biochemical validation. The October 2026 [cysteine-protease](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11143-w) and [metalloprotease](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11167-2) papers materially advance design, but their hit denominators are not the challenge's blinded endogenous-site endpoint. Attack compositional extrapolation and reporter-to-biochemical transfer before proposing candidates. An executed fixed additive baseline succeeds on random pairs but has worse RMSE than the mean on unseen multi-mutant substrate motifs; that result independently reproduced. Its below-prevalence AP interpretation changes when control eligibility matches the tested substrates, and neither computation used the authors' final barcode/backbone-corrected population. The [completed corrected-population reconstruction](archive/agents/protease-corrected-sol/report.md), freshly reproduced by the coordinator, changes that interpretation: the corrected common-key excess RMSE interval spans zero. One author-excluded backbone accounts for 54% of the original excess squared error. The original raw result is therefore not a robust corrected-data failure claim. Full endogenous folded-site accessibility and proteome-wide off-target validation remain open.

**Quadruplet reserve.** [2021 multiplex decoding](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25948-y), [2022 tolerance/cross-decoding data](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.76941), and [mitochondrial work](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.4c00630) support exact information/measurement audits. The scouts actually enumerated code maps and audited measured panels; they did not create a living code. This is the best reserve if an exact benchmark contribution is preferred to a physical model.

**Regeneration reserve.** [2026 tissue-plane source data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78607-5) were analyzed with an animal-aware injury-by-plane contrast. Intact-baseline subtraction changes effect rankings; no interaction passed the exploratory multiplicity threshold. This does not refute the paper's spatial/perturbation evidence. Pursuing new mouse targets would require raw counts, annotations, orthology, composition controls and independent cohorts.

The remaining primary evidence is documented in the three complete source-linked scout reports: [cell penetration across cell types](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschembio.4c00211), [2026 intracellular CFTR example](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-026-02199-w), [reverse-RNA extension tools](https://doi.org/10.1261/rna.080156.124), [bacterial AAV-like particles](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.2c00451), [2025 RNA copying](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-025-01830-y), [2026 small RNA polymerase ribozyme](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adt2760), [peptide-to-DNA sequencing](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-026-03061-z), [separate catalytic reverse-translation steps](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2534387123), [nitrogenase structural database](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.105613.4), and [selected dissipative peptide replication](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202518911). These are evidence for component-level opportunities and gaps, not claims that their titles establish the organizers' endpoints.

## Novelty outcome

The ranking and initial audits alone were insufficient. The accepted new contribution is the [cryopreservation thermal benchmark/counterexample](FINDINGS.md), independently reviewed using a separate Sol solver, a current primary-source/public-code search and fresh coordinator reproduction. It establishes specific criterion-dependent schedule rankings and a conditional high-loading thermal pass for the released shell. Numerical grid margin and delivery robustness are distinct; neither establishes biological recovery.

The review found prior equal-total-iron/weakest-region proposals, so those concepts are not claimed new. It also rejected an empirical triplicate inference because the dry-weight/volume moment conversion is undocumented. The accepted synthesis explicitly excludes that inference and preserves the original worker report for traceability. No equivalent of the precise numerical comparison was found in the recorded search through 26 November 2026. This supports bounded novelty, not universal first-ever priority.

The earlier Thg1 proof, raw-data Rubisco defects and within-screen protease recurrence remain qualified side results. Corrected protease data weaken the initial extrapolation-error interpretation. No improved enzyme, whole-organ recovery or complete Millennium challenge is claimed, and no public posting has been made.
