# Cryopreservation investigation audit — 27 November 2026

The 12 accepted cryopreservation records support **two investigation packages, not 12 independent scientific contributions**. Preserve the computations and their verification history. Ten records progressively test one decision: whether a simpler uniform surrounding loading can replace the released graded surround under a meaningful thermal endpoint and stress objective. Two records test temperature-window timing and its heat-capacity explanation on the same released EMG308 bag curves. These are different questions, materials and evidence, so grouping them merely because both concern cryopreservation would also be wrong.

Neither package establishes improved organ preservation. Both contain useful computational evidence. The phantom work repairs the earlier thermal-proxy limitation and produces a stronger comparison baseline plus a numerical warning. The experimental-data reanalysis supplies a limited predictive benchmark and an honest mechanistic insufficiency result.

## A: what survives

The first job reconstructs Vitrify's actual three-dimensional quasi-static Maxwell mechanics and reproduces published coarse hard peaks and smooth objectives. Its matched-iron loading ablation follows the RF history to the first coldest-heart −35 °C crossing and contrasts fixed loading versus fixed duty. That directly addresses the controlling 26 November reassessment: raising a thermal floor is insufficient to improve an uncomputed mechanical objective.

At fixed duty, detailed grading produces only a small stress difference in the tested phantom. A later jointly tuned uniform concentration and duty schedule does slightly better than the exact released graded design on the four native validation meshes under the release's rate policy. This remains a useful **feasible benchmark witness**, sufficient to require a tuned uniform comparator in future claims for spatial grading. It does not establish that the best uniform design beats the best graded design. The graded design was optimized on a different 12 s scoring horizon, the candidate search is local, and equal-budget event-objective reoptimization of both classes has not been completed.

The subsequent evidence narrows the ranking further. Different transfer and mechanical grids reverse the graded-versus-strict-uniform order. Fitting the analytic specimen boundary does not stabilize it. Common-point stress scoring exposes much larger near-corner peaks and removes the tuned uniform schedule's universal winning status on the original fitted map. A seven-block regular map suppresses those spikes but still gives opposite graded/strict orders on the two refinement levels. The tuned release-policy uniform comparator leads the regular runs, with its smallest smooth lead only 0.112 kPa. The practical decision surviving all this is **retain uniform as the simpler comparison reference, withhold a physical schedule ranking**. It is not a recommendation to change cryopreservation practice.

### Numerical correctness versus convergence

Archived verification runs and independent evaluators support reproducibility of the reported discrete calculations. They do not constitute independent experimental replication. I independently reaggregated the latest stored trajectories using only the standard library: at transfer order 20 the regular coarse graded-minus-strict gaps are +8.65864 kPa hard and +6.22091 kPa smooth; fine gaps are −2.29357 and −2.10646 kPa. On the fine regular mesh the tuned uniform lead is 0.19085 kPa hard and 0.111688 kPa smooth. These agree with the reports.

There is no demonstrated continuum error bound at those tiny paired gaps. Changing geometry, displacement space, temperature projection, phase labels, history location and score support together prevents identifying a unique cause. Large changes in absolute score do not, by themselves, bound paired comparison error; the observed paired sign reversals are the more decisive warning. Two mesh levels, affine patch tests and volume accuracy are not substitutes for convergence of the actual non-affine thermomechanical forcing and exponentially weighted objective.

The original spherified cube has an exact defect independent of the numerical optimizer. At reference corner (1,1,1), its cube-to-sphere Jacobian has diagonal 1/√3 and off-diagonal −1/(2√3), hence J(1,1,1)ᵀ=0. Subsequent radial mapping and semiaxis scaling cannot restore rank. This independent analytic invariant supports diagnosing extreme boundary strain sensitivity. It does not prove that conditioning alone explains every ranking change. The replacement regular map simultaneously changes the finite-element/thermal-history approximation.

### Objective and stopping rules

The source objective uses the log-sum-exp of all three principal stresses, with a 20 kPa smoothing scale, accumulated across elements and mechanics states. Early jobs correctly calculate this rather than a thermal proxy. Its unnormalized magnitude depends on number and placement of samples. Later shared-cloud volume weighting is a defensible diagnostic but is a different spatial aggregation from the source's cell-count score; it cannot simply be presented as a source-objective improvement. Pairwise common normalization offsets cancel, but weights and sample locations can still alter schedule order. Different event lengths also change the number of temporal samples; early peaks and the common first-12-second tests reduce that concern here.

The coldest-heart −35 °C event is grounded in the primary heart work; the hotter-than-0 °C ceiling and temperature interval of rate evaluation must be stated alongside it. The all-time 51.5 °C/min audit constraint is a benchmark choice. Its limiting state near −150 °C is below the glass transition and under extrapolated SAR. Failure there is not evidence of devitrification. The first-second grid-origin/gap/interface series removes an artificial coarse depression and approaches a simple source/capacity limit.

I independently calculate that limit at uniform −150 °C for strict uniform duty: 60×1000×1.47×1013×0.8491344654 / [(2402.3−7.6×150)(1078+0.43×150)] = **52.606052 °C/min**. The integrated minimum 52.605168 °C/min is within 0.001. Because initial diffusion vanishes identically under uniform temperature, this agreement offers little new evidence for practical rate performance beyond the analytic source/capacity balance. The gap heat-source ablation and integrated-interface comparison remain useful diagnostics of why the coarse voxel misses it.

The earliest refinement comparisons also refit the bag exponent/loading-array origin across spacings. Later fixed-shape calculations correct that confound. The fitted mechanics work changes the weak wall/exterior treatment to a free specimen boundary. Preserve those changes in any headline; this is not a single unchanged physical problem solved at progressively finer spacing.

## B: what survives

The publisher workbook supports the reported equal-loading window calculation. My separate OOXML parser and rational first-crossing interpolation reproduce direct 1 L-to-2 L seven-window MAE **0.867307 s**, against **1.972876 s** for the best tested smaller-bag constant. The warm-vial reference is weak, and its 12.74 s error must not be the primary comparator. A 1 L LAD trend performs slightly better on the paper's −100 to 0 °C span; direct-profile transfer is not the universally best tested predictor.

The heat-capacity follow-up uses the same training curves and 2 L target. It adds a meaningful mechanistic comparator to the original timing investigation rather than an independent second empirical discovery. The published proxy improves the full-span constant-power error to 1.56 s but loses to direct transfer and does not explain the late/early timing ratio after the glass transition. A conditional effective-power interpretation is valid algebra; attributing the residual to particle SAR is unsupported. Carrier-mixture heat capacity, density, boundary exchange and spatial thermometry remain confounded. The proxy's LM5 formulation differs from the water-carrier EMG308 bags.

One released curve per volume, one-second samples, dependent windows and uncertain run overlap limit prediction claims. S22B's equality to Figure 7c aggregates is evidence reuse, not replication. The new contribution is the explicit intermediate-window test and its stronger comparator, not the already published broad observation that equal loading can give similar aggregate rates across volumes.

## Focused next tests and stopping gates

1. Continue family A with one controlled numerical experiment: fixed continuous geometry, one phase-aware physical thermal reconstruction, quadrature-point Maxwell histories, well-conditioned mechanical meshes, a common physical stress-support/temporal rule, and at least a further refinement level. Test patch fields and history evolution before real trajectories. Success means paired hard and smooth differences stabilize below a declared numerical tolerance materially smaller than the claimed advantage. If the sign remains unstable at practical resolution, publish the unresolved-rank result and stop incrementing the contribution ledger for further repair steps.
2. Only after that stability gate, give uniform and graded design classes comparable optimization budgets with the same objective, SAR law, physical endpoints and rate policy. Success is a resolved objective benefit or a justified practical equivalence band across prespecified numerical/material sensitivities. The existing feasible uniform witness already justifies the stronger baseline; another grid alone does not add a contribution. Residual cooling stress can be an explicit separate physical-model sensitivity when a credible initial history is available.
3. For family B, a cheap public-data next test is to apply the locked capacity and empirical predictors to Figure 7b positions while declaring them dependent. Success would be a spatial residual pattern that discriminates a specific explanation; it cannot establish run-level generalization without lineage. Independent timestamped repeats with known loading, field, probe positions and formulation are required to estimate external timing accuracy. To distinguish heat-capacity from net-power explanations, matched mixture calorimetry/density or independently measured deposition/boundary exchange is an actual identifiability gate. More overlapping windows cannot supply those missing variables.
