## What survives and why it matters

**Input reconstruction:** the report's strongest scope is reproducibility of two archived Table 3 endpoints, not a new enzyme or proof of the historical launch configuration. Literal Version 3 metadata yields 177 S/191 KC rows; historical keys recover 183/197. The accepted report recovers all ten tuning settings with t12 and a maximum prediction discrepancy below 0.000015 log10 units; the t33 candidate fails that match. The mapped vector replacement worsens focal errors and lowers KC rank, including under archived settings. This is useful release reconstruction and a small correction-impact result. It does not establish improved prediction, the exact original cache, or independent-taxon transfer. Full embedding inference and nested fitting were not repeated by this audit.

**Relatedness/baselines:** the useful correction is to keep feasible sequence models beside embeddings and avoid interpreting a single random/grouped contrast as a causal leakage estimate. The primary grouped replay gives ESM2 pooled rank 0.720 S/0.503 KC versus trimer 0.734/0.615. Strict component purging leaves only 26–37 training rows per fold; it changes training composition and size together. Direct exclusion and matched samples show that simply counting rows is insufficient. The later group-weighted controls reveal a rank/error trade-off rather than a universal winner. Independently rescoring all final saved prediction arms reproduced their weighted rank and RMSE: component KNN group-rank tuning gives 0.469239/0.267762, while error tuning gives 0.368316/0.255801. The fixed k=3 error advantage is retrospective. Further tiny tuning variants on these same outcomes should refine this investigation, not create additional counts.

**Confirmation choice:** an independent XML workbook parse reproduced all 261 numeric candidates and 131 recorded labels. ANC_3 stays the normalized mean leader after every single-label deletion. Among 259 extant candidates, RBC2_36 loses leadership under eight deletions; the two extant leaders share no label, and their reconstructed normalized gap is only 0.0714286 s^-1 before the common multiplier. The published ANC_3 point maximum is prior work. New value lies in the assay-influence and eligibility check and the demonstrated sensitivity of extant selection. The overlap model connects the leaders but reverses its adjusted sign when Rr is included. That is evidence against a decisive model-independent extant ranking, not evidence to select a different intrinsic winner. Common calibration scaling preserves order by construction; that arithmetic itself is not a discovery.

**Peer adjustment:** independently averaging individual saved prediction errors by candidate recovered 0.2652866 log-RMSE for the direct Rr ratio, 0.2625345 for the Rr-inclusive endpoint, 0.2594666 for the tuned blend and 0.2575564 for the fixed midpoint. This confirms the claimed finite-panel ordering. It does not independently authenticate the endpoint fit or establish improvement on independent batches. The sparse-peer receipt limits the practical claim: connected singleton-peer midpoint RMSE is 0.26510, close to the direct ratio's 0.26529. Zero-peer disconnection partly follows from the chosen candidate-only support rule; the reviewer already tested an Rr-bridged alternative. A useful negative conclusion is that the full-peer gain supplies little evidence for a comparable sparse-panel benefit.

**pRR1 lineage:** this is valuable documentary reconciliation. The original K01999 record explicitly names pRR2119; its translated core, the released Rr sequence and a reported descendant core agree at 466 positions. The replacement accession and reviewed UniProt entry differ at residue 91. I reran the supplied read-only translation verifier successfully and inspected the archived sequence record and cached methods paraphrases. A fresh read of [PDB 1RBA](https://pdbj.org/emnavi/quick.php?id=pdb-1rba) confirms the His91/Asp91 discrepancy was already documented. The new increment is applying clone lineage to the historical comparator, not discovering that discrepancy. It remains inference about encoded lineage, without direct sequencing of the actual 2007 stock or characterization of mature assay proteins. Because the investigation that generated it asks whether that temperature comparator is usable, recommend it as necessary source validation there. A separate source-identity count is defensible only if the coordinator deliberately defines and counts an independent reusable clone/accession correction investigation, while removing it from the calibration count. Do not count it both ways.
