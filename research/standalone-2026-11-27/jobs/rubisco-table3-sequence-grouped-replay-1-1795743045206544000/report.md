# Table 3 retains rank agreement after grouping, and a sequence baseline scores higher

Changing the repaired Table 3 replay from row-wise folds to folds that keep identical RbcL sequences and likely conspecific records together reduced pooled Spearman from **0.753 to 0.720** for specificity (S) and from **0.598 to 0.503** for the CO₂ Michaelis constant (KC). The two rank correlations fell by 0.034 and 0.095, respectively. Both embedding models had lower error than the fold-specific training mean. A ridge model based on amino-acid trimer counts had higher rank agreement and lower error than the embedding model for both endpoints on all five grouped partitions. This benchmark supports keeping that simpler model as a comparator when evaluating claims of added predictive value from embeddings. RMSE is in log10 units. Primary mean within-fold Spearman was 0.691 for grouped S and 0.503 for grouped KC with ESM2, versus 0.753 and 0.587 with trimers. A training mean yields tied predictions within each fold. Pooling those means across folds can give nonzero Spearman through differences in fold means, so its main comparison here is RMSE. S illustrates why the two metrics should be distinguished. Grouping decreased ESM2 rank agreement while the primary pooled RMSE improved.

Across the five grouped runs, ESM2 pooled Spearman ranged from 0.712 to 0.755 for S and 0.481 to 0.530 for KC. The trimer-minus-ESM2 pooled Spearman advantage ranged from 0.015 to 0.107 for S and 0.040 to 0.112 for KC. Trimer RMSE was lower on each of those same splits. The paired random-versus-grouped ESM2 difference varied by partition and changed sign for both traits on at least one seed. The primary random-to-grouped differences therefore describe those partitions. Repeated partitions reuse the same observations and cannot serve as independent replicates.

## What changed in the evaluation

The prior feature repair reconstructed the archived ESM2-t12 feature path and substituted the Q6N0W9 vector for one *Rhodopseudomonas palustris* row. This job uses those cached vectors and independently reruns the archived fit before replacement. Maximum absolute disagreement with archived per-row predictions was 0.00001425 for S and 0.00000817 for KC in log10 units. The selected PCA dimensions and ridge penalties matched in all ten outer folds. The earlier raw species keys, duplicate weights, positive WT medians and row order are retained in both repaired arms. The prespecified grouping builds connected components before endpoint filtering. Each edge joins an exact repaired RbcL sequence match or two records with the same first two case-folded species tokens. This gives 142 represented components for 183 S rows and 155 for 197 KC rows. In the primary row-wise split, 57 S rows and 58 KC rows had a member of their component in outer training. Across the five outer training sets, each endpoint also had 202 inner validation-row appearances with a component member in inner training. All measured exposure counts were zero under grouped splitting. Standardization, randomized PCA and regression were fitted on training subsets. Hyperparameters maximized pooled inner-fold Spearman using the published grid for the ESM2 model. The trimer comparator used cosine similarities of overlapping trimer count vectors, a train-centered log target, and nested tuning of its ridge penalty on exactly the same folds. Trimer predictions were rounded to 10 decimal places before ranking to suppress floating-point differences in tied scores. The verifier checks the reported rank from the validated prediction vectors and also checks the independent trimer refit within 0.001 Spearman. No global normalization of labels or fitted sequence weighting was used.

## Closest prior work

It could not refit the released embedding model. The ledger already includes the subsequent t12 feature repair and a separate candidate-decision audit. The new comparison holds repaired inputs, labels and weights fixed and measures how the embedding model and a train-only sequence comparator perform on the same group-held-out rows. ## Scope and reproduction

The species-name heuristic can miss synonyms. Near-identical homologs, shared assay studies, short fragments and heterogeneous measurement conditions remain. Both endpoints are row-weighted measurements on this historical panel, and duplicate rows retain their influence on metrics. Split differences also reflect differences in which training records and labels are available, so they do not identify a causal penalty for duplicated sequences. An external benchmark with evolutionary and assay separation would address a different question.

From this directory, run `sh verify.sh` with the versions in `requirements.txt`. To regenerate the numerical output, run `python3 -B analysis.py` with the same environment and single-thread BLAS settings as in the shell verifier. The verifier uses the archived pure fitting functions and independently reconstructs the grouping graph, trimer kernel, kernel fits and metrics. It checks every saved prediction, tuning choice and split exposure count. Verification starts with the prior regenerated feature arrays and does not rerun checkpoint inference.
