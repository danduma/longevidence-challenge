# Fitting the specimen boundary did not stabilize the paired stress order

The graded and uniform-strict schedules swapped order on two nested mechanical meshes with the same curved specimen boundary. Both the smooth stress objective and the sampled hard tensile peak switched signs. The tuned uniform release-policy schedule still scored lowest on both measures in all four fitted runs. The outer stair-step mechanism therefore cannot be the sole explanation to investigate. Keep the tuned schedule as the benchmark reference and leave the runner-up order unresolved.

## Comparison

The strongest existing comparison used the same affine phase-overlap reconstruction on three stair-stepped mechanical grids with fixed thermal histories. Its smooth graded-minus-strict gaps were −0.238, +1.070 and −0.483 kPa at 1.4, 1.1 and 0.9 mm. The pinned Vitrify grid experiment instead varies thermal and mechanical grids together. The 2023 heart study uses finite-element mechanics with loading inherited from cooling. The 2026 artery study varies packaging and rewarming conditions and also examines maximum principal stress. Neither study tests this pair of schedules on a fixed analytic phantom. Farrell and Maddison established donor–target intersection integration for field transfer. CryoNet and Biostasis discuss uneven warming and stress through the glass transition. The Vitrify issue and pull request lists were empty at retrieval. See the attributed snapshots in [sources](sources/README.md) and the [staircase comparison](sources/staircase-report.md).

This calculation reuses the predecessor's three schedule vectors and 0.75 mm cut-cell donor histories with 0.05 s thermal steps. The extrapolated SAR and stress-free mechanical start are inherited. Each path is sampled every 0.30 s and once more at its own interpolated coldest-heart −35 °C event, between 65.822 and 65.859 s. Each schedule has 220 samples through its event. For comparison to the release objective's time window, we also score the common first 40 samples through 12 s. The functional is the release's unnormalized log-sum-exp with a 20 kPa smoothing scale over all principal-stress samples. The hard peak is the maximum sampled major principal stress. We compare schedule gaps within each mesh because the smooth score's absolute value depends on the number of spatial samples. [Pinned mechanics](https://github.com/Marc-Dvci/Vitrify/blob/1d498ee5c583e0112a762e60892d576ec2e27f57/tesseracts/mechanics/solver.py), [pinned coupler](https://github.com/Marc-Dvci/Vitrify/blob/1d498ee5c583e0112a762e60892d576ec2e27f57/coupler/workflow.py).

## Fitted calculation

Both meshes use the same analytical map of a cube to the inner cryobag superellipsoid. We use Nowell's cube-to-sphere map followed by radial scaling to the chosen superellipsoid. Every coarse reference cube splits into eight fine cubes, and the same map takes their faces and interiors into physical space. The mapped outer faces lie on the analytic specimen surface. The grids have 192 and 1,536 elements, with cube roots of mean cell volumes of about 2.25 and 1.13 mm. The exact analytic volume is 2,200.108 mm³. The stiffness quadrature gives 2,199.819 and 2,200.069 mm³. The thermal heart and chamber interfaces are resolved by same-phase integration with element phases chosen at mapped centers. [Nowell's map](https://mathproofs.blogspot.com/2005/07/mapping-cube-to-sphere.html), [pinned phantom](https://github.com/Marc-Dvci/Vitrify/blob/1d498ee5c583e0112a762e60892d576ec2e27f57/src/vitrify/geometry.py).

The transfer uses the predecessor's anchored affine reconstruction and 16 same-phase donors for each gradient. A mapped midpoint rule integrates each donor's contribution to the part of a mechanical element assigned to that analytic phase. Physical Jacobian weights and first moments account for the curved element. We use 12 and 20 points per reference coordinate on both meshes. Each transfer reproduces phase constants and the three coordinate-affine fields against its computed moments to within 3×10⁻¹⁴ °C. No quadrature weight needed the missing-donor fallback. Affine reproduction checks the reconstruction algebra. The two quadrature orders probe integration sensitivity on the actual temperature paths.

The fitted solver uses mapped Q1 displacement, three-point Gauss integration per reference coordinate, the same specimen material law and cell-center Maxwell history as the predecessor. The outer boundary is traction-free. The predecessor includes weak wall and exterior material at 10⁻⁷ of the specimen stiffness. We analytically add the free uniform dilation to the curved displacement space by removing the spatial mean only from the thermal eigenstrain, while evaluating viscosity from the original absolute temperatures. This yields an effectively zero stress for a uniform-temperature patch. Six point constraints stabilize the discrete displacement. Curved Q1 kinematics and point constraints can introduce errors even with exact boundary geometry.

## Results

All contrasts are in kPa. A positive graded-minus-strict contrast favors uniform strict. The two reference columns report the smallest of the two runner-up margins over the tuned release-policy schedule in each row.

| Mechanical mesh | Transfer points per coordinate | Smooth graded − strict | Hard graded − strict | Reference lead, smooth | Reference lead, hard |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fitted 8 × 6 × 4 | 12 | +1.324 | +1.278 | +1.676 | +1.553 |
| Fitted 8 × 6 × 4 | 20 | +1.339 | +1.294 | +1.674 | +1.553 |
| Fitted 16 × 12 × 8 | 12 | −1.125 | −1.285 | +0.795 | +0.590 |
| Fitted 16 × 12 × 8 | 20 | −1.119 | −0.951 | +0.804 | +0.903 |

The 12-point smooth contrast moves by 2.449 kPa on refinement. Increasing the transfer order shifts the smooth contrast by at most 0.016 kPa and the hard contrast by at most 0.334 kPa. The hard peaks occur between 7.8 and 8.4 s, and the common 12 s window gives the same order on all four runs. The archived scores include the full outputs for both horizons. [Results](results.json).

The comparison shows that a signed reversal can occur with a fixed curved boundary and the same donor histories. It cannot apportion the earlier Cartesian reversal among boundary geometry, cell averaging, local phase selection, spatial resolution and stress sampling. The fitted and Cartesian solves also differ in the treatment of the weak wall and exterior. Two fitted levels and two overlap quadratures give a finite sensitivity result. A continuum ranking, between-solve differences in the physical peak, and biological fracture risk remain unresolved.

## Reproduce

Run `sh verify.sh` with Python 3.12 or later. The script uses an existing NumPy/SciPy environment or prepares a local one with the pinned versions. It reconstructs the donor histories from the cached input vectors and code, reruns all 12 fitted solves, independently forms signed gaps from the absolute scores, and checks the signs, quadrature sensitivity, reference leads, sampled boundary geometry, analytic volume, patch diagnostics, event times and archived scores. The archived Cartesian comparison supplies the baseline contrasts. `python3 -B analysis.py` regenerates the fitted archive in an environment with NumPy and SciPy. Attribution and original file versions are in [source provenance](sources/README.md).
