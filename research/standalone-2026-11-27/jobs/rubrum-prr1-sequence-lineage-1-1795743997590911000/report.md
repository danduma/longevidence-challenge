# The pRR1 temperature series has a sequence match supported by clone lineage

McNevin et al. (2007) assayed Rubisco expressed from pRR1 and cite Morell et al. (1990) for the plasmid. Morell et al. describe transferring the structural gene from pRR2119 and restoring initiation at the native Rubisco start. The Somerville deposit ATCC 37846 cross-references K01999, and the original source note in K01999.1 explicitly identifies the DNA as coming from pRR2119. This is a specific clone link. The translated insert matches the 2025 GitLab control Rr and its stated RefSeq identifier WP_011390153.1 at all 466 positions. [McNevin et al.](https://biology-assets.anu.edu.au/CMS/FileUploads/file/Farquhar/258McNevin3Rubiscos.pdf) · [Morell et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1016/0014-5793%2890%2980879-N) · [ATCC 37846](https://www.atcc.org/products/37846) · [K01999.1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/K01999.1) · [2025 Rr release](https://gitlab.com/milo-lab-public/rubisco_is_slow/-/blob/e1c9ed945864a0744e81b9cfdbfeb609ccf67907/data/rubisco_library.csv)

GenBank reports that X00286.1 replaced K01999.1 in 2003. ENA lists K01999 as a secondary accession. Treating the alias as sequence-preserving would incorrectly assign His91 to the clone-linked archived sequence. Independent translation locates the nucleotide discrepancy at CDS position 271, the first base of codon 91. The records establish a disagreement and give no explanation for it. [GenBank archive](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=K01999&rettype=gb&retmode=text) · [ENA](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/api/embl/X00286) · [UniProt](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04718.txt)

A documented descendant corroborates Asp91 away from the engineered terminus. Mueller-Cajar and colleagues took the gene in pTrcrbcM from pRR1 and replaced its first five amino acids. Frey used pTrcrbcM as template and lists a fusion with the native start restored and a 20-residue C-terminal extension. After removing that extension, the reported core matches the 2025 Rr sequence. The restored first five residues do not provide independent evidence about what was in pTrcrbcM, and Frey’s reported fusion sequence supplies no direct read of the McNevin vial. [Mueller-Cajar et al., methods](https://www.academia.edu/116793068/Directed_Evolution_of_Rubisco_in_Escherichia_Coli_Reveals_a_Specificity-Determining_Hydrogen_Bond_in_the_Form_II_Enzyme) · [Frey thesis, pp. 105, 107](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/bitstream/handle/20.500.11850/282703/1/180805_E-version_PhDthesis_RaphaelFrey.pdf)

## How to use the historical series

The convergence supports a sequence-matched external temperature comparator by documented lineage. Direct sequencing of the 2007 working stock and terminal characterization of the assayed proteins remain unavailable in the inspected sources. Morell and Mueller-Cajar already established parts of the lineage, and both are cited in the released Rr row. That row lists four 25 °C literature entries and omits McNevin. The prior coordinator audit already digitized McNevin’s approximate 25-to-30 °C contrast and identified the pRR1 construction paper. The increment here is the clone-specific sequence reconciliation, including the misleading replacement accession and the independent descendant branch. The GONUTS specialist annotation entry points to the generic UniProt and ENA records and supplies no assay-stock bridge. [GONUTS](https://gowiki.tamu.edu/wiki/index.php/RHORU%3ARBL2)

The historical temperature contrast holds the radiometric format fixed within the McNevin series. Across studies, McNevin used XL1-Blue, conventional purification and frozen storage, radiolabeled carboxypentitol binding to count active sites, and radiometric CO₂ fixation. The 2025 Form II methods describe BL21(DE3), a cleavable His-SUMO fusion with nickel capture and protease elution, CABP inhibition titration, and NADH-coupled spectrophotometry in a gas-controlled plate reader at 30 °C. The construct comparison concerns the encoded core. These differences leave the temperature response of the 2025 preparation and the separate assay-format effect unmeasured by this bridge. [McNevin et al.](https://biology-assets.anu.edu.au/CMS/FileUploads/file/Farquhar/258McNevin3Rubiscos.pdf) · [de Pins et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12663927/)
