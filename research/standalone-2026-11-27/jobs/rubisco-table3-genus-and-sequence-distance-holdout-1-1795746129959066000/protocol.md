# Matched holdout protocol

Freeze the primary exact-sequence/likely-species GroupKFold test assignments from the preceding replay. Keep the same labels, row weights and repaired embeddings. Start with the same five outer and four inner split procedure. In each new arm, remove from the training side every row in a component represented on the validation or test side, without changing the outer test rows. Build the graph across all benchmark rows before endpoint filtering, with the prior exact/likely-species edges in every arm.

Prespecified additions for this analysis are literal genus (first case-folded whitespace token of the historical species key), and joint genus plus global alignment identity at 99% and 95%. The latter cutoffs represent near-identity and a stricter barrier and are benchmark definitions, not biological clade boundaries. Align complete available FASTA strings end to end, including any fragments in the released inputs. Biopython PairwiseAligner global mode uses +2 match, −1 mismatch, −5 gap opening and −1 gap extension (same for terminal gaps). Take the first optimal alignment in lexicographically sorted unique-sequence order. Identity is matching columns divided by all columns, including terminal and internal gaps. Thresholds are inclusive. Transitive paths can connect rows below the direct identity cutoff. Nested purging applies the arm's graph to each validation fold after outer exclusion. Record any unavailable arm rather than silently switching its split or relaxing its cutoff.

Use the same train-only ESM2 PCA/ridge and normalized-trimer kernel ridge grids and pooled inner Spearman objective. Include the training mean and an alignment-identity nearest-neighbor comparator with k in 1, 3, 5, 10, taking all neighbors tied at the kth distance. Report log10 RMSE, pooled rank and mean within-fold rank, alongside training coverage and residual nearest training identity. Treat fold metrics and differences as descriptive. Explicitly test the direct exclusion boundaries at both CV levels.
